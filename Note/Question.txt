Problem:

Write a Python program that takes a product name, 
product price and quantity from the user. 
Calculate the total price and display the shopping information.

Requirements:
1. Take product name as input.
2. Take product price as input.
3. Take quantity as input.
4. Convert price into Float and quantity into Integer.
5. Calculate the total price.
6. Convert the total price into Integer using Type Casting.
7. Display all information using print().
